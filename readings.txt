Core methodology papers 

Engle (2002) — "Dynamic Conditional Correlation: A Simple Class of Multivariate GARCH Models" — Journal of Business & Economic Statistics. Your main winning estimator. Read fully.
DeMiguel, Garlappi & Uppal (2009) — "Optimal Versus Naive Diversification: How Inefficient is the 1/N Portfolio Strategy?" — Review of Financial Studies. Your evaluation framework and your result echoes theirs. Read fully.
Patton & Sheppard (2009) — "Evaluating Volatility and Correlation Forecasts" — Handbook of Financial Time Series. Your Frobenius norm and forecast evaluation methodology. Read sections 1-3.
Bates & Granger (1969) — "The Combination of Forecasts" — Operational Research Quarterly. Short, read fully. Theoretical grounding for BMA.
Diebold & Mariano (1995) — "Comparing Predictive Accuracy" — Journal of Business & Economic Statistics. The actual DM test paper you just used. Read sections 1-3.

Covariance estimation 

Ledoit & Wolf (2004) — "A Well-Conditioned Estimator for Large-Dimensional Covariance Matrices" — Journal of Multivariate Analysis. Your LW estimator.
Ledoit & Wolf (2022) — "The Power of (Non-)Linear Shrinking" — Journal of Financial Econometrics. Updated version, cite alongside 2004.
Bollerslev (1986) — "Generalized Autoregressive Conditional Heteroskedasticity" — Journal of Econometrics. The original GARCH paper, cite for GARCH(1,1) within DCC.
Markowitz (1952) — "Portfolio Selection" — Journal of Finance. Cite for MVO foundation, you know what it says.

Regime detection

Hamilton (1989) — "A New Approach to the Economic Analysis of Nonstationary Time Series and the Business Cycle" — Econometrica. Your HMM. Read sections 1-3.
Ang & Bekaert (2004) — "How Regimes Affect Asset Allocation" — Financial Analysts Journal. Closest empirical precedent to your regime-conditional evaluation.

Eigenvalue / random matrix theory 

Laloux et al. (1999) — "Noise Dressing of Financial Correlation Matrices" — Physical Review Letters. Why most eigenvalues are noise, why top eigenvalues matter. Short paper, read fully.
Ledoit & Wolf (2004) already covers this — their shrinkage motivation is explicitly about eigenvalue correction so you don't need a separate citation for most of the eigenvalue intuition.
Fan, Fan & Lv (2008) — "High Dimensional Covariance Matrix Estimation in Approximate Factor Models" — Annals of Statistics. Shows portfolio optimization depends on systematic factor eigenvalues. Read introduction and section 2.

Portfolio construction 

Maillard, Roncalli & Teïletche (2010) — "The Properties of Equally Weighted Risk Contributions Portfolios" — Journal of Portfolio Management. Your risk parity objective function.
Jagannathan & Ma (2003) — "Risk Reduction in Large Portfolios: Why Imposing the Wrong Constraints Helps" — Journal of Finance. Your long-only weight constraint justification.
Sharpe (1966) — "Mutual Fund Performance" — Journal of Business. Original Sharpe ratio, cite for your evaluation metric.

Bayesian model averaging

Raftery, Madigan & Hoeting (1997) — "Bayesian Model Averaging for Linear Regression Models" — Journal of the American Statistical Association. Formal BMA grounding.
Timmermann (2006) — "Forecast Combinations" — Handbook of Economic Forecasting Ch. 4. Why combinations sometimes fail, directly relevant to your negative result on adaptive BMA.

Factor models

Harvey (1989) — "Forecasting, Structural Time Series Models and the Kalman Filter" — Cambridge University Press, Ch. 2-4. Your Kalman filter factor model.
Stock & Watson (2002) — "Forecasting Using Principal Components from a Large Number of Predictors" — Journal of the American Statistical Association. PCA initialization for your factor model.

Backtesting discipline:

Lopez de Prado (2018) — "Advances in Financial Machine Learning" — Wiley. Ch. 2 (data), Ch. 7 (walk-forward), Ch. 12 (backtesting). Don't read cover to cover — just these three chapters.

The disconnect finding — supporting literature:

Patton (2011) — "Volatility Forecast Comparison Using Imperfect Volatility Proxies" — Journal of Econometrics. Formally treats the forecast accuracy vs realized performance disconnect.
Kan & Zhou (2007) — "Optimal Portfolio Choice with Parameter Uncertainty" — Review of Financial Studies. Shows estimation error affects portfolio performance in ways forecast accuracy metrics don't capture.
